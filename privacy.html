Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

At RajGan Group, we respect your personal and business data. When you submit a project inquiry, request a quotation, or communicate via our forms or WhatsApp, we collect information such as your full name, email address, phone number, company name, and project requirements.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information collected is strictly utilized to evaluate your software requirements, provide project estimates, sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and deliver requested software development services. We never sell or share your information with third-party marketers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We employ industry-standard SSL encryption, secure AWS server infrastructure, and strict internal access controls to ensure your project files, source code, and personal details remain confidential.

4. Contact Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at support@rajgan.in.
